# Registry provenance and historical replies

Only curated registry rows with `seededBy: "seed"` may establish an organization's identity or supply its domains, phones, or policy quotes to verification and replies. Search-name similarity is insufficient proof of ownership. Unknown-organization search and scrape still run, but their results are stored only as internal, family-scoped proposals with `seededBy: "firecrawl"`. They do not enter extraction aliases, attach to cases, or participate in checks or the registry refresh job. Family-added rows are also unreviewed. No public review, provisioning, or onboarding endpoint is added.

Candidate writes require an existing family, use a family-specific key in the resolver, and cannot overwrite reviewed seed rows or another family's cached candidate. Curated seed data remains the authority; this guard does not independently re-verify the provenance of every previously stored seed row.

A historical case pointing to an unreviewed or missing registry row is projected with `sourceReviewRequired: true`, no official name or evidence, and `cannot_verify`. An old detached case with a stored organization name is treated the same way. Stored drafts and provider receipts remain unchanged as audit records. Sending an unaccepted reply is blocked before any model or email request. Re-resolution and rechecking also refuse to rewrite a historical case with saved reply material, so they cannot hide the review requirement while retaining an outdated draft. Cleanup refuses to delete referenced candidate rows.

Recovery requires an explicitly authorized data review before any production mutation. Inventory affected cases and preserve their evidence, exact draft text, provider message IDs, timestamps, and idempotency history. Confirm each source independently against reviewed registry data, then reconcile any attempted send against provider acceptance before deciding whether another message is permitted. Never clear a receipt, swap draft text under the same send key, or relabel a scraped candidate as reviewed merely to resume sending. Recheck unaffected fresh cases normally; recovering a historical saved reply needs a deliberate migration or replacement-case procedure that preserves the original audit record. That recovery procedure and any resend require separate review and authorization.

This change performs no live data migration, source promotion, email send, or deployment. Existing accepted messages cannot be recalled. Local synthetic regressions cover candidate lookup, cross-family cached rows, seed resolution, protected writes, historical projections, and send blocking.
